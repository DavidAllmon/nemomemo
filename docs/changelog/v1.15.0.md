## What's new
- **Pick how long Dory remembers.** A Dory memo can now forget itself after 1 hour,
  24 hours, 3 days, or 7 days — pick the window right in the editor or the ⋯ menu.
  And a plain edit no longer restarts her countdown. 🐟
- **"Dory is about to forget this memo."** One hour before a memo fades away, a heads-up
  lands in your inbox with a one-click **Keep it** button. No more silent goodbyes.
- **Nudge me about this.** Ask to be reminded about any memo at a time you pick — it
  arrives in your inbox (and by email, if your reef sends mail). Nudges can repeat
  daily, weekly, or monthly: "every Monday: water the plants." 🔔
- **Message in a bottle.** The opposite of a Dory memo: seal a memo away and it stays
  hidden — even from you — until the day you chose, then washes ashore in your inbox.
  A note from past you. ⏳
- **Dory's Memory page.** A new **Dory** entry in the sidebar shows everything currently
  fading (soonest first), your bottles still at sea, and her lifetime count: "Dory has
  forgotten 214 memos for you." 🫧

## Technical notes
- Migration 0005: `memo.remind_at`, `memo.remind_every` (DAILY/WEEKLY/MONTHLY CHECK),
  `memo.surface_at` (+ partial indexes), `user.dory_forgotten_count`, and an inbox
  rebuild extending the type CHECK with `REMINDER`, `BOTTLE_ARRIVED`, `DORY_WARNING`.
- New `services/scheduler.ts` replaces the standalone dory sweeper in both entry points:
  one minute-tick that surfaces bottles, fires reminders (recurring ones advance from
  the due slot with single-nudge catch-up past downtime), emits deduped expiry warnings,
  then runs the dory sweep (which now bumps per-user forgotten counters). Cloud runs the
  tick per open reef.
- Feed queries gain a second time guard beside the Dory one:
  `surface_at IS NULL OR surface_at <= now`. Pending bottles are creator-only in
  `checkMemoRead`/`canGlimpseMemo` — share tokens included; the share route now goes
  through the ACL chokepoint instead of an inline dory check.
- `POST/PATCH /api/v1/memos` accept `doryWindow` ('1h'|'24h'|'3d'|'7d'), `surfaceAt`,
  `remindAt`, `remindEvery`; `assertTimeRules` enforces pinned⟂dory, pinned⟂bottle,
  and dory+bottle ⇒ `forget_at > surface_at`. New `GET /api/v1/memos/dory` (fading +
  bottles + counter) registered before `/:uid`. `MemoDto` gains `remindAt`,
  `remindEvery`, `surfaceAt`; export frontmatter stamps `surfaces:`/`reminds:`.
- Web: `/dory` page + sidebar entry, Dory window dropdown + bottle dialog in the
  editor, reminder dialog in the ⋯ menu, three new inbox renderings with one-click
  rescue. 16 new server tests (144 total); migration rehearsed against a seeded
  pre-0005 database.
