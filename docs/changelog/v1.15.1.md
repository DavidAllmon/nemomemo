## What's new
- **Sealing a bottle is now one tap.** The message-in-a-bottle dialog swaps the fiddly
  date-and-time field for tide presets — tomorrow, in a week, in a month, in a year —
  plus a proper calendar for any other day. It tells you in plain words when your memo
  returns ("Washes ashore Sunday, August 30 · in 7 days"), and a little bottle drifts
  further from shore the further out you throw it. 🌊

## Technical notes
- New `web/src/components/memo/BottleDialog.tsx` extracted from MemoEditor: preset
  chips compute local 09:00 targets (calendar-aware month/year adds), custom path is
  native `<input type="date">` + compact time field, validity gates the Seal button
  with a reef-voiced past-date error, and the drift line is log-scaled
  (`log(days+1)/log(731)`) behind `motion-safe` transitions. No API or schema changes.
