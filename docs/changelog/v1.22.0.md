## What's new
- **A calendar for your reef.** The new Calendar page lays your memos out month by
  month, with the first line of each one right there in the day it was written. Click
  a day to read — and edit — everything from it; leave it unpicked and you get the
  whole month as a timeline.
- **On this day.** Home now quietly resurfaces what you wrote a month, six months or a
  year ago today. It only shows up when there's something to find, and it folds away
  if you'd rather just write.
- **Streaks.** A small line under the mini-calendar counts the days you've written in a
  row, and remembers your best run. Nothing appears until you've got one going. 🔥

## Technical notes
- New `web/src/lib/time-travel.ts` is the single home for local-calendar math —
  `localDayKey`, `dayRange`, `monthRange`, `anniversaryAnchors` (month-end and
  leap-day clamped), `anchorsToFilter`, `streakFrom` (DST-safe: a day boundary is
  anything under 36h) — with a 16-case vitest suite. `CalendarHeatmap` dropped its
  private `dayKey` copy in favour of it.
- **No new server surface.** Both features are ordinary memo-list queries carrying
  `created_ts` range filters, so the Dory guard, the bottle guard, the comment
  exclusion and creator scoping are all inherited from `buildMemoListWhere`. The
  timezone stays in the browser — same reasoning as the `displayTime` filter chip —
  which keeps historical DST offsets correct where a fixed server offset would not.
- `/calendar` is a lazy route with its own Vite chunk (5.8 KB); state lives in the URL
  (`?month=YYYY-MM&day=YYYY-MM-DD`); one query per month capped at `MAX_PAGE_SIZE`
  (200) with a footer note when a month overflows. `MemoListParams` gained an optional
  `pageSize`.
- `AppShell` widens its content column to `max-w-5xl` on `/calendar` only; every other
  route keeps the `max-w-2xl` reading column.
- "On this day" shows at most 2 anniversaries × 2 memos and renders nothing at all when
  empty; collapse state persists in `localStorage` under `nemo-on-this-day`.
