## What's new
- **Tend your tag garden.** A new **Settings → Tags** page lists every tag you use.
  Rename one and it changes in every memo you own — nested tags included. Rename it
  onto a tag you already have and NemoMemo offers to merge them into one.
- **Renames are never a leap of faith.** Every memo a rename touches keeps its old
  words in **⋯ → History**, so you can always look back — or swim back.
- **Paint your tags.** Pick from eight reef colors per tag; your tag chips and the
  sidebar wear them everywhere. Colors are yours alone — reef-mates see their own.

## Technical notes
- Code-health #1 closed: `TAG_REGEX`/`MENTION_REGEX` now exported from shared (the
  one tokenizer) with a new anchored `isValidTagName()`; the web copy and the rename
  route's looser validation regex are gone (`a/`, `a//b`, `-a` now rejected).
- `POST /users/-/tags/rename` captures a `memo_revision` per rewritten memo inside
  the same transaction, and migrates `tagColors`/`pinnedTags` keys to the new name
  (target's own color wins on merge). Merge is rename-onto-existing; no new route.
- `userGeneralSettingSchema` gains `tagColors` (record of tag → one of 8
  `TAG_COLOR_NAMES`, capped at 200 entries, defaults `{}`) — no migration needed.
- Web: tag palette OKLCH variables in all three theme blocks; `lib/tag-colors.ts`
  maps names to static Tailwind classes; chips (MemoContent) and sidebar glyphs
  (TagTree) read the viewer's colors; Settings gains the Tags section and the old
  Preferences rename card is removed.
