## What's new
- **Your account now has an anchor.** New accounts sign up with an email
  address, and you can sign in with your username *or* your email — whichever
  you remember first.
- **Prove it's really you.** On reefs with email set up, you'll get a friendly
  verification link when you join or change your address. A little badge in
  Settings shows when you're verified, and a gentle banner reminds you if
  you're not.
- **Already here without an email?** Nothing breaks — you'll just see a nudge
  to add one so your account can always be rescued. (Self-serve password reset
  arrives in the next release, and it needs a home address to swim to.)
- **New cloud reefs start rescuable.** The claim page now asks for your email
  and fills it in from your checkout automatically.

## Technical notes
- `services/email.ts`: `Mailer` interface + nodemailer SMTP impl from
  `NEMOMEMO_SMTP_HOST/PORT/USER/PASS/FROM`; `trySend` is fire-and-forget with
  logged failures. All email features no-op cleanly when env is unset (the
  self-host-without-mail mode). Injected via `makeApp(db, config, {mailer})`.
- Migration `0004_email_identity.sql` (additive): `user.email_verified_ts`,
  `auth_token` (purpose CHECK, sha256 token_hash, single-use, expiring).
- `signupRequestSchema` requires a trimmed/lowercased email; uniqueness is
  app-layer and case-insensitive; signin falls back to a unique email match;
  email change resets verification and re-sends; removing a set email is
  refused. `/auth/verify` + rate-limited `/auth/verify/resend`.
- Cloud: `StripeGateway.getCustomerEmail`, claim form email field (prefilled),
  claim credentials validated with the full signup schema.
- `InstanceProfileDto.emailEnabled`; web: signup email field, verify page at
  `/auth/verify`, AppShell nudge banner, Settings verified badge/resend.
- `deploy/seed-demo.mjs` seeds emails. Suite: 101 server / 27 web / 27 shared.
