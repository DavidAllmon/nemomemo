## What's new
- **Forgot your password? No more panic.** There's a "Forgot your password?"
  link on the sign-in page now — enter your email, click the link we send, pick
  a new password. One hour, one use, done.
- **Invite your reef-mates properly.** Reefkeepers add a member with just a
  username and email — the new member gets a friendly invite and picks their
  own password. No more whispering temporary passwords across the dinner table.
- **Your account watches its own back.** Change your password or email and
  you'll get a heads-up message — so if it ever *wasn't* you, you'll know
  immediately.
- **Cloud niceties**: your claim link now arrives by email the moment you pay,
  and if a payment ever fails you'll get a gentle note before anything sleeps.

## Technical notes
- `POST /auth/forgot` (rate-limited 5/hr/IP, always 200 — no enumeration) and
  `POST /auth/reset` (PASSWORD_RESET token: 1h reset / 7d invite; sets hash,
  marks email verified, deletes all user sessions). Web: /auth/forgot,
  /auth/reset (invite=1 variant), sign-in link gated on emailEnabled.
- Admin create (POST /users): email required; password optional ⇒ invite path
  (unusable random hash + `sendInviteEmail`); 400 without SMTP and no password.
- Account PATCH: `passwordChangedMessage` to self, `emailChangedMessage` to
  the previous address.
- Cloud: `BillingDeps.mailer` (platform SMTP), `claimLinkMessage` at
  provisioning to `getCustomerEmail`, `dunningMessage` on
  invoice.payment_failed; claim's internal signup now sends
  `host: <slug>.<baseDomain>` + x-forwarded-proto so emailed verify links
  resolve (fixes broken-origin links for claim-created admins).
- Suite: 109 server / 27 web / 27 shared.
