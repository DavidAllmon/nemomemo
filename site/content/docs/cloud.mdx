Don't want to run a server? **NemoMemo Cloud** is the same software, hosted for you:
your own private reef at `your-name.trynemomemo.com` for **$1.99/month or $19/year**.
See [pricing](/pricing) for the current details.

## How it works

1. **Try the demo first.** There's no trial — the [live demo](https://demo.trynemomemo.com)
   *is* the try-before-you-buy.
2. **Check out with Stripe.** Card handling is entirely Stripe's; we never see your number.
3. **Claim your reef.** Right after payment you pick your subdomain and create the first
   account — you're the reefkeeper (admin). The whole thing takes about a minute.
4. **Invite your school.** Members, visibility, comments, Dory — everything in these docs
   works exactly the same on Cloud.

Keep the claim link from your checkout page safe until you've claimed — it's also stored
on your Stripe receipt's customer record if you lose it.

## What's different from self-hosting?

Almost nothing — that's the point. The differences:

- **We run it**: updates, monitoring, and nightly off-server backups are our job.
- **Isolation**: every reef is its own separate database. Other customers' reefs are
  physically invisible to yours.
- **Fair use**: memos are unlimited; each reef includes up to 25 members and 5 GB of
  attachments. These are abuse brakes, not upsells — ask if you need more.
- **Billing lives in Settings**: reefkeepers get a **Billing** tab with subscription
  status and the Stripe portal for card changes and cancellation.

## Leaving (we'll miss you)

Cancel anytime from Settings → Billing. Your reef stays up through what you've paid for,
then sleeps for 90 days (data intact, sign-in blocked) before it's permanently deleted.
Resubscribe in that window and it wakes right up. And since NemoMemo is free to self-host, you
can always export your data and [self-host](/docs/deploy) the same reef yourself.

See the [Terms of Service](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for the fine print.


## If a payment lapses

Your reef never disappears without warning. A failed payment emails you and marks
the reef **past due** (everything keeps working — update your card in Settings →
Billing). If the subscription fully lapses, the reef **takes a nap**: visiting it
shows a "Wake it up" button that reopens checkout on your existing account — fix
the payment and everything is exactly as you left it.

Napping reefs are kept for **90 days**, then deleted for good (that's the promise
in our terms — your data doesn't linger after you've left). Want your memos out
before then? Settings → Backups gives you the full download any time.
