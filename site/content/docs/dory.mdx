Some notes only need to live a day: the parking spot, the confirmation code, the errand.
Dory memos let you say so up front, and then never think about them again. And Dory's
department has grown: she can also **nudge** you about a memo at a time you pick, or carry
a **message in a bottle** that stays hidden until its day arrives.

## Dory memos: how it works

1. Open the **Dory** button (the little blue tang 🐟) in the editor — at creation, or
   later from a memo's action menu — and pick a forget window: **1 hour, 24 hours,
   3 days, or 7 days** (24 hours is the classic).
2. The memo shows a countdown badge: **"forgets in 23h"** (or "3d" for the long windows).
3. Over its final six hours, the card gently fades.
4. When time is up, the server deletes it — truly deletes it, along with its comments,
   attachments, and share links.

The timer runs server-side, so forgetting happens even when nobody has the app open.
Re-picking a window on an existing memo starts a fresh countdown; choosing **Off**
cancels it. Plain edits don't touch the clock.

### The warning shot

One hour before Dory forgets a memo, a notice lands in your inbox: *"Dory is about to
forget this memo"* — with a one-click **Keep it** button that cancels the countdown.
Anxiety becomes trust. (A 1-hour Dory memo gets its notice right away — accurate, if
a little eager.)

### The rules

- **Pinned memos can't be Dory memos.** Pinned means "keep forever"; the two are mutually
  exclusive, and the app will refuse the combination with a friendly error.
- **Archiving rescues.** Archive a Dory memo and its countdown is cancelled permanently —
  archive means "keep."
- **Comments sink with the ship.** Commenting on a Dory memo is fine, but when Dory forgets
  the memo, the conversation goes too. The comment box warns you.
- **No recovery after zero.** Forgotten is forgotten. That's the feature.

## Reminders: "Nudge me about this"

Any memo can carry a nudge. Open its **⋯ menu → Nudge me about this**, pick a time, and
Dory taps you on the shoulder when it arrives: an inbox note, plus an email if your
instance sends mail. Nudges can repeat **daily, weekly, or monthly** — "every Monday:
water the plants." Pair it with Dory for the full move: *remind me, then forget it.*

If your instance was asleep when a recurring nudge came due, you get **one** catch-up
nudge, not a pile.

## Message in a bottle

The inverse of Dory: instead of forgetting a memo, hide it until a future day. Press the
**Bottle** button (⏳) in the editor and pick a tide — **tomorrow, in a week, in a month,
in a year** — or choose any day on the calendar. The dialog tells you in plain words when
it returns ("Washes ashore Sunday, August 30 · in 7 days") while a little bottle drifts
further from shore the further out you throw it. Sealed, the memo goes to sea — invisible
in every feed, even yours, until its day arrives. Then it washes ashore as an inbox note:
*a memo from past you.*

- A bottle at sea is **yours alone** — nobody else can see it, share links included,
  until it surfaces.
- Bottles can't be pinned, and comments can't be bottled.
- A bottle can also be a Dory memo, as long as it surfaces *before* she'd forget it.
- Changed your mind? Open the bottle dialog again and **pull it back ashore**.

## Dory's Memory page

The **Dory** entry in the sidebar opens her memory: everything currently fading (soonest
first), your bottles still at sea, and her lifetime count — *"Dory has forgotten 214
memos for you."* 🫧

## Changing the default window

Admins can shorten or lengthen Dory's default memory instance-wide with the
`DORY_TTL_SECONDS` environment variable (default `86400`). Setting it to `60` is a fun
way to watch her work. The per-memo window picker always offers 1h / 24h / 3d / 7d.
