## Writing

The editor speaks GitHub-flavored Markdown: headings, **bold**, *italic*, ~~strikethrough~~,
lists, task lists, fenced code blocks with syntax highlighting, tables, images, and links.
The toolbar splices Markdown at your cursor; **⌘+Enter** saves. Drafts persist in your
browser, so a closed tab never eats a thought.

Interactive checkboxes work in the rendered memo too — tick `- [ ]` items right in the
timeline and the source updates itself. The **Tasks** submenu on a memo can check or
uncheck everything at once.

### Templates

The **Template** button in the compose box starts a memo from a skeleton: four
built-ins (daily journal, standup, meeting note, recipe — dates fill themselves in),
plus up to 20 of your own. Write something once, choose **Save current memo as
template…**, and it becomes a one-tap starting point. Templates are per member.

## Visibility

Each memo carries one of three levels, chosen in the editor:

| Level | Who can read it |
| --- | --- |
| Private | Only you |
| Protected | Any signed-in member of your instance |
| Public | Everyone, when the instance is in public mode |

Set your default in **Settings → Preferences**.

## Pinning

**Pin to top** keeps a memo above the timeline. Pinned memos can't be Dory memos or
bottles — pinned means forever, Dory means a while.

## Time tricks

Three moves live in the editor and the **⋯ menu**, covered in depth on the
[Dory & the time layer](/docs/dory) page:

- **Dory memo** — forgets itself after 1 hour, 24 hours, 3 days, or 7 days.
- **Nudge me about this** — an inbox (and optional email) reminder at a time you pick,
  one-shot or repeating.
- **Message in a bottle** — hidden from every feed until a future day, then it washes
  ashore in your inbox.

## Archiving vs. deleting

Two different intentions, two different places:

- **Archive** — *keep it, out of the way.* The memo moves to the treasure chest: out
  of your timeline, safe forever, restorable any time. Archiving a Dory memo rescues
  it from being forgotten.
- **Delete** — *get rid of it,* with a week to change your mind.

## The trash

Deleting a memo moves it to **Trash**, where it waits 7 days before it's really gone.
Comments and attachments travel with it, and they all come back together when you hit
**Restore** — an archived memo restores to the archive, not to your timeline.

While a memo is in the trash it's gone from everywhere else: your timeline, search,
tags, the calendar, and any share links you'd made. Its permalink says the memo swam
away, even to you.

Each entry shows how long it has left. **Delete forever** skips the rest of that week
for one memo, and **Empty the trash** clears the lot right now — both are permanent,
and both ask first.

Dory memos don't pass through the trash. A memo you asked Dory to forget fades on
Dory's schedule — that's the whole point of asking.

## Edit history

Every save keeps the words it replaced. **History** in the **⋯ menu** lists a memo's
past versions — the last 20 edits, for 90 days — with a line-by-line diff against what
the memo says now, and a one-click **Restore this version**. Restoring keeps today's
words in the history too, so you can always swim back.

History is yours alone: only the memo's author can open it, even on a public memo.
And when a memo is gone — trashed, or forgotten by Dory — its history goes with it.

## Acting on many memos at once

**Select**, above your feed on Home and in the archive, turns on multi-select: click
memos to pick them, then use the bar at the bottom — **Archive** (or **Unarchive** in
the archive), **Add tag…**, or **Move to trash**, up to 100 at a time. Bulk-tagging
adds the tag at the end of each memo and skips ones that already carry it; every memo
it rewrites keeps its old words in **⋯ → History**. Bulk delete goes through the same
7-day trash as deleting one memo — comments travel along.

## Finding your way back

Two views exist purely for re-reading what you wrote:

- **Calendar** lays a month out as a grid with the first line of each memo in the day it
  was written. Click a day to read and edit everything from it; leave the day unpicked
  and you get the whole month as a timeline. The mini-calendar in the sidebar links
  straight to the month you're looking at.
- **On this day** sits above your feed on Home and resurfaces what you wrote a month,
  six months, or a year ago today. It only appears when there's something to find, and
  it folds away.

**Go fish** in the sidebar swims up one random memo. The small flame under the
mini-calendar counts the days you've written in a row.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `c` | Write a new memo |
| `/` | Search your reef |
| `⌘K` / `Ctrl K` | Search |
| `j` | Next memo in the feed |
| `k` | Previous memo |
| `e` | Edit the focused memo |
| `Enter` | Open the focused memo |
| `Esc` | Let go of the focused memo |
| `?` | The cheat sheet |

Every one of these rests while you're typing — inside the editor, a search box, or any
dialog, the keys belong to what you're writing. Inside the editor, **⌘+Enter** saves.
