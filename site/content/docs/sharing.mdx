---
title: Sharing
description: Visibility levels for ongoing access, share links for handing someone a memo.
---

NemoMemo separates two ideas: **visibility** (who can always see a memo) and
**share links** (a URL that opens one memo for anyone, regardless of visibility).

## Visibility

Set per memo in the editor: **Private** (you), **Protected** (signed-in members), or
**Public** (everyone, when the instance is public). Public memos appear on the Explore
feed and your profile.

## Share links

From a memo's menu, choose **Share…** and create a link. Share links:

- work for anyone, signed in or not — even for private memos,
- expire after **1 day, 7 days, 30 days, or never** (your choice),
- expose only the memo, its attachments, and its reactions — never comments or the
  memo's connections,
- can be revoked at any time from the same dialog.

A share link to a Dory memo dies when she forgets it, whatever the link's own expiry.

## Public mode

The instance-wide **Public mode** switch (Settings → Reef) controls whether anonymous
visitors can see anything at all. Turn it off and Explore, profiles, and public memos all
require sign-in; share links keep working, since handing someone a link is always an
explicit act.
