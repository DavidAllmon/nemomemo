## Comments

Any memo you can read, you can comment on — full Markdown included. Comments inherit the
parent memo's visibility, and the author is notified in their inbox. (Comments on
comments aren't a thing; the reef keeps conversations one level deep.)

## Reactions

Tap the smiley on any memo to react from the instance's emoji set. Tap your reaction
again to remove it. Admins curate the set in **Settings → Reef** — yes, you can add 🦀.

## Mentions

Write `@username` in a memo or comment and that member gets an inbox notification linking
back to it.

## Inbox

The bell in the sidebar shows your unread count. Notifications cover comments on your
memos and mentions of you; archive them one at a time or **Mark all read**. If the memo
behind a notification has since been deleted (or forgotten by Dory), the notification says
so gracefully.
